the idea

Feed reading is a list, a list, and a body of text — the exact shape a terminal is good at. Tide keeps your subscriptions, the headlines, and the article on one screen, and moves through all three from the keyboard.

where the family began

The one the rest are built on

Tide came first. Its layout and theming became a toolkit, and the toolkit became a family.

Tide

The three-pane layout, the live theme-preview workflow, and the soft modal language were designed here, for reading feeds.

→ tideui

Those primitives were pulled out into tideui, a themeable multi-pane view layer for Bubble Tea and Lipgloss.

→ TideMail

Refined in a keyboard-first email client — unified inbox, undo-send, GUI setup.

→ TideFTP

And again in an SFTP / FTP transfer client — a real queue, conflict policies, directory mirror.

→ WhatTheDock

And a Docker client for containers and Compose across hosts.

Learn Tide's keys and you already know how to drive the other three. See the whole family →

the reader

What it does

Three panes, one screen

Feeds and folders on the left, headlines in the middle, the article on the right. Opening a piece never hides the queue behind it.

A unified unread view

Everything new across every feed in one list, or drill into a single feed or folder.

Articles as clean text

The reading pane renders each item as readable terminal text — no browser, no chrome.

Keyboard throughoutj k · o

Move through headlines with j/k, open with o, cross panes with Tab. ? for a searchable key list.

Search your feeds/

Filter headlines as you type; the query stays live while you move through what matches.

Themes that stay readableT

The same contrast-checked palettes as the rest of the family, previewed live before you commit.
